## Brand & Style
The design system is crafted for children, parents, and elementary educators in Portugal. It embraces a **Tactile Playful / Gamified** movement, blending soft organic shapes inspired by puzzle pieces with Duolingo-like tactile button states. The visual tone is intensely welcoming, joyful, warm, and zero percent corporate. 

Interactions evoke curiosity, cheerfulness, and accomplishment. Micro-interactions feel bouncy and tangible—simulating physical wooden/plastic educational blocks, smiling sticker badges, and soft pill surfaces that build confidence in young learners and reassure parents.

## Colors
The palette balances vibrant, friendly chromatic tones with creamy, gentle backgrounds that prevent eye fatigue during study sessions:

- **Primary (`#7C6BF0`)**: A warm, welcoming lilac-lavender that serves as the visual anchor for main actions, active navigation, and puzzle mascots.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: An energetic sunflower yellow/amber representing streaks, rewards, achievements, and morning routines.
- **Tertiary (`#58CC02`)**: A vivid gamified green for success feedback, lesson completions, and progress bars.
- **Neutral (`#475569`)**: A soft slate-charcoal providing comfortable reading contrast without harsh black tones.
- **Named Accents**: Peach/coral (`#FF7A59`) for creative subjects, Sky Blue (`#1CB0F6`) for math/science modules, and Cream (`#FBF9FF`) for full-canvas grounding.
- **Tactile Shadows**: Saturated, darker undertones (`#6454D6`, `#D97706`, `#46A302`) formulate bottom borders (`border-b-4`) to produce Duolingo-style push-down 3D clickability.

## Typography
Lexend provides unparalleled readability and open letterforms, designed specifically to reduce visual stress and accelerate reading comprehension for school-age children and early readers. 

Headlines feature punchy, friendly weights (Bold 700 and ExtraBold 800) with soft curves that match the rounded design language. Body text leverages regular and medium weights with generous line-heights to ensure instructions and learning exercises are effortless for both kids and parents.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a fluid, touch-first responsive grid. Children and parents interact predominantly through tablets and smartphones:
- **Mobile (< 640px)**: 4-column layout with 16px margins and 16px gutters, maximizing tappable target dimensions (minimum touch zone 48x48px).
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with 32px margins, prioritizing card-based modular activities side-by-side.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column constrained fluid grid (maximum content width 1140px) centered against the soft cream canvas.

Spacing is generous and uncluttered. Elements breathe comfortably to reduce cognitive overload and maintain an open, sunny rhythm.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids cold, corporate blur drops in favor of **tactile 3D volume and soft ambient pill floats**:
- **Tactile 3D Slabs**: Interactive action buttons and gamified tiles use physical extrusion created with a thick bottom border (`border-b-4` or `border-b-[5px]`) matching a darker tone of the element's hue. On active press, the button translates downward (`transform: translateY(3px)`), flattening the bottom border to simulate a real-world arcade button.
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards sit on the `#FBF9FF` background supported by warm, tinted ambient drop-shadows: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(124, 107, 240, 0.08)` and `0 2px 6px 0 rgba(71, 85, 105, 0.04)`.
- **Playful Bubbles & Badges**: Floated milestone items utilize a mild pastel outline (`2px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`) paired with soft colored glows.

## Shapes
The design adopts maximum organic roundedness (Level 3 - Pill-shaped). Sharp corners are strictly prohibited:
- Buttons, chips, navigation pills, and interactive tags are fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- Feature cards, lesson containers, and modal sheets use ultra-curved geometry (`rounded-3xl` / 24px to 32px).
- Illustrative masks incorporate interlocking puzzle contours, rounded starbursts, and friendly smiling blob silhouettes inspired by Edulite and Duolingo.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary 3D Pill**: Lilac background (`#7C6BF0`), white bold text, with a `border-b-4 border-[#6454D6]`. On `:active`, translateY by 3px with `border-b-0`.
- **Reward / Action Pill**: Vibrant Green (`#58CC02`) with `border-b-4 border-[#46A302]` or Yellow (`#F59E0B`) with `border-b-4 border-[#D97706]`.
- **Ghost / Neutral Button**: White background with a `2px solid #EDE9FE` border and `border-b-4 border-[#DDD6FE]`.

### Cards & Lesson Tiles
- Built with a white surface, `rounded-3xl`, and padded by `space-lg`.
- Activity header modules feature pastel-tinted illustrations (e.g., lavender puzzle character, coral paint palette).
- Lesson list cards feature rounded icon containers (e.g., circular play buttons) paired with clear completion status icons.

### Chips & Subject Filters
- Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`) with subtle pastel fills (e.g., `#EDE9FE` for Math, `#FFE5DF` for Arts).
- Unselected chips feature soft muted labels; active chips gain a vibrant primary background with bold white typography and a subtle spring scale animation.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Inputs feature `rounded-full` boundaries with a soft creamy interior (`#FFFFFF`), a generous `16px` vertical breathing room, and a friendly 2px focus ring tinted in lavender (`#7C6BF0`).
- Placeholders are phrased warmly and familiarly (e.g., "O que vamos aprender hoje?").

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Scaled up for motor accessibility (28x28px minimum).
- Custom circular and rounded-square outlines with thick 3px borders. Checked state activates a lively bouncy pop displaying a white checkmark against Duolingo-green (`#58CC02`).

### Gamification & Badges
- Floating streak counters with animated yellow sun/flame icons.
- Puzzle-piece progress bars featuring rounded inner progress indicators and celebratory confetti feedback upon completion.
